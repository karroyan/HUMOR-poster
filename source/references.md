# Primary-source poster references — inspected 2026-10-19

## 1. Attention, Learn to Solve Routing Problems! (ICLR 2019)
Author source: https://wouterkool.github.io/pdf/poster-attention-learn-to-route.pdf
Publication listing: https://wouterkool.github.io/publication/
Visual inspected from full local rendering: /tmp/ref-attention.png (downloaded public original PDF /tmp/ref-attention.pdf).
Award status: no best-poster award claimed for this poster.

Actual visual observations: Landscape, compact full-width title, white base, asymmetric 2-column upper grid which becomes three blocks below. Soft rectangular backgrounds divide functions. Top-left problem statement moves immediately into notation and sample diagram. Top-right compares four problem variants side by side; this occupies broad horizontal space because comparison demands it. Lower-left architecture uses actual encoder/decoder figures and explanatory bullets. Training block pairs REINFORCE equation and pseudocode with short explanatory annotations. Results include a learning curve AND a very dense baseline table. References small at bottom. This is unequivocally a technical research poster with much more information than a sparse marketing page.
Lessons to transfer: variable block widths driven by content; equations + semantic labels; methods deserve large diagrams; quantitative table and compact plot can coexist; brief concrete headings make dense content navigable. Do not transfer the tiny full-paper table, clip-art lightbulbs, or excessive soft colored panels literally.

## 2. Stochastic Beams and Where to Find Them (ICML 2019)
Author source: https://wouterkool.github.io/pdf/poster-stochastic-beam-search.pdf
Publication listing: https://wouterkool.github.io/publication/
Visual inspected: /tmp/ref-stochastic.png (original /tmp/ref-stochastic.pdf).
Award status: author's listing says ICML 2019 Honorable Mention for the paper, NOT Best Poster.

Actual visual observations: Very wide dark forest-themed canvas tied to paper title. Left strip provides preliminaries, center features an enormous probability tree with attached equations and a stepwise top-down sampling sequence. Algorithm pseudocode and experiments occupy right strip. Long technical content is structured around a worked example; labels sit directly on the diagram. The poster has personality because theme relates to the actual scientific title, rather than generic branding.
Lessons to transfer: a worked example dominates and connects formal method, algorithm, and evaluation; technical visual hierarchy can coexist with rich content; primary visual is explanatory, not decorative. For HUMOR use real meme semantics as personality, while keeping white background, restrained palette, and legible sans-serif type. Avoid dark photographic background/parchment motifs, which hurt print readability.

## HUMOR redesign recommendation
Three scientific columns: (1) Task, dataset, and hierarchical-CoT training with a real worked example; (2) pairwise within-template comparison, EBC/ranking, policy objective and restrained guarantee; (3) a readable full main result table, reward rank plot, generalization, and limitations. A horizontal qualitative comparison strip can span the lower part, annotated with what changed in the joke. Short meaningful numbered headings; 700–1000 words where required by details; real data captions; white background, dark neutral text, one deep teal for methods and one muted ochre for the key distinctions. No oversized slogan, pill badges, gradient cards, empty hero block, or invented result claims. This is a research discussion surface, not a launch page.
