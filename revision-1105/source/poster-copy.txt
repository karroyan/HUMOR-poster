$\mathbb{E}_{r\sim P_\theta}[\tilde h_G(r)]\geq 1-(1-\alpha)\delta$
Good-path mass α > 0; mean humor gap ≤ δ.
INFERENCE  Explore/select; no gold caption.
Compare within a template; learn a group ranking.

boring, detailed boring, and generated memes.

$H(\pi_\theta)\geq H(\pi_{\mathrm{CoT}})-\sqrt{\Delta_{\mathrm{KL}}/2}$
